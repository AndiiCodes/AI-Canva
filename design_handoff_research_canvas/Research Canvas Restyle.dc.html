What changed
Try the Tweaks panel to see the not-run state and full transcripts. Theme rows and safety flags are clickable.
1. Lighter group frames
Solid yellow and blue backgrounds are now pale outlined frames with a small label, so the boxes stand out.
2. One colour per step, used sparingly
Blue, violet and teal appear only on each step’s number tile, ports and data. Amber and red are kept for friction and risk, so the safety step is no longer red.
3. Cleaner headers
Numbered tiles replace emoji and show the order. Dropped “Box”, the duplicate type label and the ×. The second line shows the result (“5 themes · from 3 transcripts”) and a pill shows status.
4. Transcript excerpts
Participant boxes show three lines instead of a wall of text. Full text is still available.
5. Themes ranked
Sorted by mentions with a small bar. Rows expand to show supporting quotes.
6. Journey as a chart
The stage list is now an emotion curve, with the three friction stages grouped in one amber band.
7. Safety flags as a review list
All five flags visible with severity. One opens at a time with its tags, affected stage, reason, evidence trace (stage → theme), read-only quote and Approve / Dismiss. The bar fills as you review.
Journey stages and UX Coach
Stages still expand to show the description, linked theme and quote. Coach recommendations open to why it matters and numbered next steps, with Approve / Dismiss.
8. Quieter run controls
Full-width coloured Run buttons replaced by a small Rerun next to the token count. Before running, each box explains what it does and what it waits for.
9. Straight connections
Ports sit on the header line, so the three AI steps connect in one line. Neutral grey lines.
10. Type
Instrument Sans for UI, IBM Plex Mono for labels and numbers, larger body text.
